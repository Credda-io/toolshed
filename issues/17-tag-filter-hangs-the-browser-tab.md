**Title:** A tag filter pattern hangs the whole portal, not just my tab

Was trying to find everything tagged with a bracket in it and typed this into
the sidebar tag filter:

    (a+)+b

The page never came back. That would be fine, except my colleague at the next
desk was on a different machine and the portal stopped answering for her too,
for about a minute, until somebody restarted something.

So whatever it does with that box, it does it somewhere shared.

The help text says `*` is the wildcard, so maybe brackets were never meant to
work at all -- but "does not work" and "takes the site down" are different
things.
